Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026 (draft)

Who we are

The Peak Form app is published by Peak Form ([company name and address to be confirmed]). Your coach (for example Younes Method) uses it to coach you.

Your coach decides how your coaching data is used (the “controller”). Peak Form runs the app and stores the data for your coach (the “processor”), and is responsible for keeping it secure. [Roles to be confirmed in the coach agreement.]

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email, date of birth, sex, height and weight.
  • Health and fitness data (sensitive personal data): injuries, health conditions, medication, pregnancy or breastfeeding, sleep, heart rate, steps and workouts from Apple Health or Health Connect if you connect them, check-ins, body measurements, progress photos, pain reports, and the meals and workouts you log.
  • Messages and files you send your coach, and basic technical data needed to run the app (device type, app version, error reports).

Why we use it, and on what basis

To build and adjust your nutrition and training plan, let your coach follow your progress, keep you safe (for example flagging serious pain to your coach), book calls, and run and improve the app.

We process your health data only with your explicit consent, which you give when you sign up. You can withdraw it at any time (see “Your rights”); your coaching then stops, because the plan depends on it.

Who sees your data

Only your own coach sees your health data. Peak Form staff access it only to support you or fix a problem, and only as needed.

Service providers that help run the app process data on our instructions under contract: database and hosting (Supabase), email delivery (Resend), and phone notifications (Apple and Google). [List to be confirmed by legal review.] We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in the European Union (Ireland). This means it is transferred outside the UAE. We do this only with appropriate safeguards as the UAE Personal Data Protection Law requires [mechanism to be confirmed by legal review].

How long we keep it

While your plan is active, and for 30 days after it ends so you can still view your history. After that it is archived and deleted within [period to be confirmed], unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account) or on our account deletion page. You confirm with your password. We then delete your login, profile, sign-up answers, plans, check-ins and photos, workouts, meals, pain reports, chat and calls, and your coach is told.

Your coach keeps a record of your past payments for their accounts (amount, date, plan length), without your name or other personal details, for as long as accounting law requires [period to be confirmed].

Your rights

You can ask to see your data, get a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to its use, and withdraw your consent. Ask in the app (“Something wrong? Tell us”) or by email at support@peakform.ae. We reply within [30] days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UAE Data Office.

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit, each coach can only reach their own clients, and access is logged. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Age

Peak Form is for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.

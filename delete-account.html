Delete your Peak Form account

You can also do this in the app: Settings → Delete account.

What is deleted

Your login, profile and sign-up answers, plans, check-ins and photos, workouts, meals, pain reports, chat and calls. This cannot be undone.

What is kept

Your coach keeps a record of your past payments for their accounts (amount, date, plan length), without your name or other personal details. Your coach is told that you deleted your account.

Delete now

Sign in with the email and password you use in the Peak Form app.

Your account is deleted. Your login and personal data have been removed. Thank you for training with Peak Form.

Can’t sign in?

Reset your password in the app (“Forgot password?”), or email support@peakform.ae from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm it is you, then delete it within [30] days.

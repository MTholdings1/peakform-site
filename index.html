Peak Form is a private coaching app. Personal trainers use it with their own clients: your nutrition and training plan, check-ins and chat with your coach, all in one place.

Training

Your coach’s workouts with videos. One tap per set, and it works with no connection in the gym.

Meals

Meal options with swaps your coach approved. Log a meal with one tap, no counting.

Progress

Check-ins every two weeks, photos and measurements your coach reviews with you.

Your coach

Chat and calls with your own coach. Serious pain you report goes straight to them.

How to join

Peak Form is by invitation only. Your coach emails you a personal code; download Peak Form from the App Store or Google Play and enter it to create your account.

Peak Form does not give medical advice. There are no purchases in the app: you arrange payment directly with your coach.

For coaches

Interested in using Peak Form with your clients? Write to support@peakform.ae.
